Diversity hiring

Proactive about diversity, equity and inclusion.

As specialists in diversity talent research and intelligence, we partner with global brands to help them meet their diversity hiring goals and targets.

We do this by mapping the market for you and delivering pipelines of diverse talent exclusively for you.

  1. Market researchDetailed reports on diversity in your industry and location.
  2. Talent mappingWe identify top diverse talent and build pipelines for your internal talent team.
  3. Talent screeningIf your team is stretched, we screen candidates and deliver a shortlist of interested, relevant diverse talent.
  4. Candidate experienceWe act as the link with your internal talent team so candidates stay engaged with your company.
  5. Embedded talent partnerOne of our experts sits alongside your internal team to make sure you hit your goals.
  6. Inclusive process reviewA deep dive into your recruitment process, including adverts and interview questions.
